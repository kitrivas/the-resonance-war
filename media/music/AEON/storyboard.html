Concept and through-line

One ring runs the whole film and tightens with each act: first the corona of an eclipse, then an ouroboros eating the sun, then the accretion disk of a singularity. The audience watches the same shape grow hungrier until it takes AEON. The final frame returns to the eclipse ring that opened the film, so the video closes on itself the way the serpent does.

  • Act I — Eclipse over the Worlds (0:00–1:33). AEON on five living, mostly evacuated worlds hung close in one sky under an eclipse; tilted frames, rolls, leaps between worlds; light drains upward into the corona.
  • Act II — The Library (1:33–2:10). Kairo alone in a vertical shaft of floating light-scripted tablets; the first stillness in the film; the tablets converge on the ouroboros glyph.
  • Act III — Ouroboros (2:10–2:28). The red reveal on the 2:10.2 808; full-group choreography on a ring platform while a band of light consumes the sun in steps.
  • Act IV — The Unmaking (2:28–3:12). Five members, five dying worlds, intercut faster as the build climbs.
  • Act V — Singularity (3:12–4:05). Reunited, hovering in the debris field; at 3:21.6 the event horizon blooms and they launch at it; the dive at 3:42; time stops at 3:51; swallowed at 4:00.1.

The serpent is never a snake. It reads as a serpent only through motion: segmented pulses of light travel around the band like scales, and one dense, brighter region visibly feeds on the band's own trailing end. No head, eyes or fangs appear anywhere.

Grade progression. The world stays gray throughout: ash, pewter, slate, with silver highlights in Act I and gray-blue in Act II. Red enters at 2:10.2 as the only saturated colour and stays on the costumes alone through Acts IV and V. The one earlier warm note in the film is Kairo's amber eyes in Act II.

Horizon rule. The frame is canted or rolling for most of the film. It goes level only three times: Shiyun's slow orbit at 1:05, the library hush at 1:42.8–1:51.8, and the 3:51.0 void. A level horizon becomes the audience's cue that time has stopped.

The three looks are silver-white for the group in Act I, oracle white for Kairo alone in Act II, and red with black accents for Acts III to V. Every surface is high-gloss: patent, lacqué leather, liquid lamé, polished chrome hardware. Each look sits inside the member's canon palette and labels.

Camera and edit grammar

The tilted-frame vocabulary has three grades, and the cut rate follows the bar grid: 0.58 s per beat, 2.32 s per bar.

Tilt grades

  • 15° cant for unease: verses, walks, the approach shots.
  • 30–45° cant for action: leaps, the Act III wide, Jisu's collapsing city, the Act V flight.
  • Full rolls (180° or 360°) for transitions and for the world turning around a still performer: shots 4, 7, 15, 22, 41.
  • Rotated 90° or inverted frames for gravity changes. In them the performer stays the fixed point and the world is wrong.
